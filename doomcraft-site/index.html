What's real, and what isn't yet

open alpha
Real: four modes, rigged characters with an avatar editor, synthesised audio grounded on the real DOOM WAD, online multiplayer with an authoritative server, a server-granted XP/Scrap economy (dark by default while it's tuned), and a player profile. Source is public.
  • Not yet: items and skins, trading, seasons and tournaments, the sponsor platform. Specced, not shipped.
  • No ads today. The slots exist; nothing fills them.
  • Progress: the online build saves on the server by device; accounts are new and optional.
