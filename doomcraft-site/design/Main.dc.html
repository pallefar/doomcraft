Why it feels different

Doom speed, not Minecraft speed
~9.5 m/s, instant acceleration, air-strafe, one-block step-up. Other voxel shooters walk.
Gunfeel
Muzzle flash, kick, screen shake, hit markers, a live crosshair. The competition's viewmodel doesn't move one pixel.
Arenas, not scenery
Rockets reshape the map. Build cover in Horde, then watch it get chewed.
Your phone, either way up
Portrait and landscape, a real fire button, auto-fire and aim assist. The bar tells you to rotate your screen.
